Frequently asked questions

How do I create an account?
Open EVA, tap Sign in, then Sign up, and follow the steps. You can also activate an invited account.

How do I make a reservation or booking?
Browse Explore to find a venue, event, stay, or experience, then follow the prompts to reserve or book. You can track the status of each booking in the app.

Where are my tickets?
Open the Wallet tab to see your tickets and QR passes. Show the QR code at the door for entry.

How do I add friends or create a gathering?
Use the Social tab to add friends with your personal QR code, and the Gatherings tab to create or join a plan.

How do I manage privacy or delete my account?
Open Profile → Settings to manage privacy options, or contact us at the email above to request account deletion.
