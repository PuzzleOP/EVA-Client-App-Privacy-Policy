Delete Your EVA Account

App: EVA  ·  Developer: KVERTI DOO

You can request deletion of your EVA account and the personal data associated with it at any time, using either method below.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the EVA app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Settings.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deactivated immediately and your personal data is removed as described below.

Option 2 — Request by email

Email contact@qwerty.com.mk from the email address on your EVA account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will verify your request and process it within 30 days.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove the personal data linked to it, including:

  • Your profile information (name, username, email, phone, and profile details).
  • Your reservations, bookings, tickets and QR passes.
  • Your gatherings, friend connections, and in-app messages.
  • Your saved venues and preferences.

What data may be retained

We may retain a limited amount of data where we are legally required or permitted to do so, such as:

  • Transaction and invoice records needed for accounting, tax, and legal compliance, retained for the period required by applicable law.
  • Records necessary to prevent fraud, resolve disputes, or enforce our terms.
  • Aggregated or anonymized data that can no longer identify you.

Timing

Personal data is deleted within 30 days of a verified request, except for the limited records described above, which are kept only for as long as the law requires and then deleted.
